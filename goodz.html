How Along With Goodz Works
⚡ The "Z" 3-Leg Relay Geometry
Instead of paying ₹3,500+ to book a whole truck for a small box, Goodz links 3 stages into the letter "Z":

• Top Bar (🏍️ Bike): Local Captain picks up from your doorstep & drops at the partner bus counter.
• Diagonal Slash (🚌 Bus): High-speed passenger bus carries your parcel across NH 163 highway in 2.5 hours.
• Bottom Bar (🛵 Bike): Destination Captain delivers to the receiver's doorstep (or self-pickup for ₹349!).
🚌 How Does the Captain Know Bus Space?
The bike captain never wanders around looking for buses. They deliver directly to our designated partner counters (TSRTC Cargo / Orange Travels Office) where buses depart every 15-20 minutes. Staff registers the parcel onto the next departing bus within 60 seconds!
