RYDEALOT Privacy Policy

🗑️ Request Account & Data Deletion

In compliance with the Google Play Store User Data Policy and Indian Digital Personal Data Protection Act (DPDP Act 2023), you may permanently delete your Rydealot account and associated personal data at any time.

⚠️ What will be permanently erased:
• Profile information (Name, Email, Verified Phone Number)
• GPS ride history & parcel dispatch timestamps
• Uploaded driver documents, DL, RC, Aadhaar & vehicle data (for captains)
• In-app wallet tokens and customer promo badges
Note: Financial transaction receipts required by Indian tax laws (GST/TDS) will be archived for statutory retention periods only.

✅ Deletion Request Received

Your request has been logged. In accordance with safety policies and Google Play standards, your personal profile, GPS tracking data, and account records will be permanently purged within 48 to 72 hours.
