Why Operating as a "Parcels Only" Network is 100% License-Free

In India and Telangana, commercial bike-taxis carrying passengers face regulatory hurdles (Central Aggregator Guidelines, commercial yellow-board requirements, and transport union pushback).

Courier, Parcel, and Goods Delivery (like Swiggy Genie, Dunzo, Porter, or Rydealot Sage) have NO passenger aggregator license requirement! A private two-wheeler or linehaul carrier can legally transport parcels, documents, food, and cargo with zero transport department bans.
